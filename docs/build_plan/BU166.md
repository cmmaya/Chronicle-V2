# BU166 — Enable / Disable Diarization In The Session Menu

Status:
Completed

Goal:
Turn diarization on and off from a session card's "•••" menu in the All
Sessions window.

Context:
- The menu is built by `_build_session_actions_menu` (src/app/window.py).
  Its look comes from `_ALL_SESSIONS_MENU_QSS` in the pixel appearances
  and from the theme hook in Boring Corporate.

Allowed Files:
- src/app/window.py (`_build_session_actions_menu` and its handlers)
- tests/test_bu166.py (new)
- docs/build_plan/BU166.md
- docs/build_plan/index.md
- docs/current_state.md
- docs/devlog.md

Dependencies:
Required:
- BU165

Blocks:
- BU169

Tasks:
- One menu item, after the Transcribe / Summarize item:
  - not enabled and not running: "Enable diarization"
    - first run: a confirm pop-up saying it finds who spoke when, runs in
      the background and takes about 10-15 min per hour of audio (plus a
      one-off ~46 MB download if the models are missing)
    - already diarized (status 'ready'): switches on at once, no pop-up
  - running: "Cancel diarization"
  - enabled: "Disable diarization"
- "Disable diarization" opens a small pop-up, "Use the original
  transcript, without speakers?", with **Use original transcript**
  (primary) and Cancel. Confirming switches the session back to the
  original transcript.
- Disabled items explain why, like the existing ones:
  "Enable diarization (after recording stops)", "(transcribe first)".
  Hidden for inserted-transcript sessions (no audio).
- After a failed run the item reads "Enable diarization (retry)", with
  the error in its tooltip.
- If the session is open in the main window, its transcript reloads when
  the switch happens.
