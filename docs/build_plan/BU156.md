# BU156 — Core Widgets

Status:
Completed

Goal:
The shared widgets in `pixel_widgets.py` take the corporate shape:
flush panels, flat navigation rows, pill and ghost buttons, rounded tail-less
bubbles, sentence-case titles, thin window buttons.

Context:
- Engine-level restyling (BU153) already covers fonts, borders and corner
  paths; this BU handles what is structural.

Dependencies:

Tasks:
- `PixelPanel`: a top-level panel is the black canvas (no border); an inner
  one a hairline rounded box, or nothing after `set_flat(True)`.
- `PixelSectionTitle`: 12px grey sentence case (`_sentence_case`), also on
  `setText`.
- `PixelButton`: sidebar -> flat 38px nav rows; otherwise a dark pill whose
  radius `_PillStyle` keeps at half the height (Qt squares any radius over
  that); `accent="primary"` -> white pill, black text. `PixelToolButton`:
  ghost circle, 20px icons. `corporate_button_qss(kind, selector, height)`
  for fixed-height buttons; `PRIMARY_BUTTON_QSS` becomes a white pill.
- `PixelBubble`: no tail, 18px (user) / 14px radius, no border except find
  (muted) or selection (`selection` role); variant `"plain"` = text on the
  canvas (falls back to `"blue"` in pixel themes). Find chips use one
  dark-on-light pair. `PixelScopePrompt` rounded, no tail.
- Conversation list: one-line regular title + small date in flat rows.
  Section headers flat, hover grey. Group headers, group labels, gap
  separators and spaced captions in sentence case. Session cards: Open is a
  white pill, ••• a ghost circle, the side stripe only for live sessions.
- `PixelWindowButton`: thin 10px line glyphs, grey hover, Windows-red close.
