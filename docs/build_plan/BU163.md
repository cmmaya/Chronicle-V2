# BU163 — Diarization Engine

Status:
Completed

Goal:
Turn a session's stored audio into speaker turns ("who spoke when"),
with progress and cancel, using the configuration the 2026-10-11 test
validated.

Context:
- sherpa-onnx's own clustering is not usable as-is: it reported 19
  (WeSpeaker) and 140 (TitaNet) speakers on the 2-speaker DR session 2.
  The working result came from re-clustering sherpa's segments:
  TitaNet embeddings, average-linkage cosine clustering at distance 0.6,
  clusters under 5% of talk time treated as noise. Same setting gave
  2 speakers (55% / 42%) on DR session 2 and 5 on Article Critique.
- Session audio is many WAV chunks per source (`audio/mic`, `audio/system`;
  ~5 s live chunks, ~27 s imported ones), with silences already cut.
- Measured cost: 0.16-0.22x real time on 4 threads (31 min for 142 min).

Tasks:
- Per source with audio, concatenate its chunks (sorted, 16 kHz mono)
  and keep an offset map: concatenated time -> (chunk file, absolute time).
- Segment with sherpa (pyannote segmentation-3.0); embed each segment of
  1 s or more with TitaNet (first 10 s); cluster with average linkage,
  cosine distance threshold 0.6 (module constant).
- Clusters under 5% of the source's talk time are not speakers: their
  segments join the nearest kept cluster's centroid. Segments under 1 s
  take their neighbour's speaker.
- Merge adjacent turns of the same speaker (gap under 1 s).
- Number speakers across the session in order of first appearance
  (Speaker 1, 2, ...); mic and system speakers are numbered separately
  (no cross-source matching).
- `diarize_session(session_path, progress, cancel_event) -> list[Turn]`,
  Turn = (source, speaker, start_abs, end_abs, chunk spans).
  `progress(stage, done, total)`; cancel checked between chunks/segments.
- 4 threads, leaving cores for live transcription.
